Workshop Materials
================

You may access the workshop materials in the [**data**](../data/) folder
of this repository. This folder contains the following files:

1.  **2015_Tree_Census_Manhattan.csv**: trees in Manhattan
2.  **2018_Central_Park_Squirrel_Census.geojson**: squirrels in Central
    Park
3.  **Bike_Routes.geojson**: bike routes in New York City
4.  **Borough_Boundaries.geojson**: boundaries of NYC boroughs
5.  **NYC_Parks.geojson**: boundaries of NYC parks
